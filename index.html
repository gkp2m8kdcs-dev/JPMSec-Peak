The peak of heat-transfer fundamentals, in your pocket.

JPMSec Peak is a focused calculator and reference for the classical dimensionless numbers of convective heat transfer — Nusselt, Prandtl, Reynolds, Grashof, Biot and more. Designed for students, instructors and practising engineers who want quick, trustworthy answers without leaving the device.
