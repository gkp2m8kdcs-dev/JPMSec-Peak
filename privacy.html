Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This privacy policy applies to the JPMSec Peak mobile application (the "App") developed and maintained as an offline educational reference and calculator for heat-transfer fundamentals. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data Collection

JPMSec Peak does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The application runs entirely on your device. No accounts, no profiles, no identifiers.

2. Network Access

The App does not initiate any network requests. No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or third-party trackers are embedded. Nothing leaves your device.

3. Local Storage

Your preferences (display units, decimal precision, chart style, accessibility toggles) are stored locally in iOS UserDefaults and never leave your device. You can reset them at any time by uninstalling or by toggling the relevant options in Settings.

4. Permissions

JPMSec Peak requests the following iOS-protected resources only when you actively use the corresponding feature, and only on your device:

  • Photo Library (read & write) — used when you save a snapshot of your calculation results to your photo library, or when you attach an existing image as calculation context.
  • Camera — used when you snap a photo of a hand-written calculation, a piece of equipment, or a labelled diagram to attach as context to the current calculation.

All processed images stay on your device. The App does not upload photographs, calculation snapshots, or any other content to external servers. You may revoke either permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos / Camera.

5. Children

The App does not target children and does not knowingly process data of any user.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be reflected in the App and on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Because no data is collected, prior versions of the policy have no effect on any historical data.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer:

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. JPMSec Peak is fully offline. All formulae, typical values, and your saved settings live on your device.

Where are my preferences stored?

In iOS UserDefaults on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them; reinstalling starts you at the defaults.

Why does the app ask for Photos or Camera access?

Only when you actively save a snapshot of your calculation results, or attach an image as context. The app never uploads these images — they remain on your device.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the App Store. Use Apple's "Report a Problem" page on your purchase history to request one.

How do I reset all settings to their defaults?

Open Settings in the app and toggle each option off and on, or simply delete and reinstall the app.
